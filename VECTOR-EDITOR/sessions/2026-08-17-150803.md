## Decisions

- **GitHub Pages is the primary share link, not the artifact.** Pages serves `text/html` with no
  sandbox, so all four exports download with real extensions. The artifact needs a share click and
  cannot save `.pdf`/`.dxf` at all — the host filename allowlist is
  `gif png jpg jpeg webp mp4 webm txt json md` plus an extended set, and neither is on it. The
  artifact is now redundant for sharing and can be ignored.
- **`.nojekyll` rather than leaving Jekyll on.** A Jekyll failure unpublishes the *entire* site, not
  just the offending page, and `sessions/*.md` in the repo made that a live risk. Nothing here is a
  Jekyll site, so there was no upside to keeping it.
- **The `.txt` fallback for refused extensions was kept** even though it is ugly. A file the
  recipient renames beats a button that silently does nothing. Only matters inside the artifact;
  Pages never hits this path.
- **Did not force-push to fix a bad commit message.** See open threads — left for the user to decide
  rather than rewriting pushed history unattended.
