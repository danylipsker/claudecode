How to play

Divide the grid into regions so that every region contains exactly one dot and is symmetric under a 180° rotation about that dot. Every cell must belong to a region. A dot may sit on a cell, on an edge or on a corner; the cells touching it always belong to its galaxy.

Painting (recommended)

  • Click a dot to pick its galaxy, then click or drag over cells to claim them. The mirror-image cell is filled for you.
  • Drag straight from a dot into the grid to claim cells on the way.
  • Click a painted cell to pick up its galaxy; click a cell of the current galaxy again to clear it. Right-click clears too.
  • A region that is closed, symmetric and holds one dot lights up. Light up the whole board to win.

Walls

  • With the Walls tool, click or drag along grid lines to draw region borders, just like the Nikoli and Simon Tatham versions. Click again to remove.
  • Walls and paint work together: borders between differently painted cells count as walls.

Helpers

  • Hint explains one logical next step and can apply it for you.
  • Check marks anything that contradicts the solution.
  • Undo and Redo step through every move.

About

Galaxies was invented by Nikoli under the name Tentai Show (天体ショー). It appears in Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection, contributed by James Harvey, and on Android through Chris Boyle's port. Puzzle codes here use the same game-ID format, so puzzles can be exchanged both ways. Every generated puzzle is verified to have a single solution.
