Quaternions — 3D rotations, composed & interpolated

q = w + x·i + y·j + z·k, i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1. Rotation of v: p' = q·p·q⁻¹ (unit q). Composing rotations = multiplying quaternions — order matters, same as matrices. Everything here also works in 4×4 mode: quaternions are applied into the composite matrix and slerp-interpolated to the shape.
