# Course plan — Israel, Hebrew first

Chapter ids and order are in `course.json`. Each entry: chapter id — Hebrew
title — lesson-id prefix, then the lessons (id: subject) and what must be
covered. Writers may split or merge lessons if the material asks for it, but
must cover everything listed and nothing that belongs to another chapter
(link to it instead with `[[chapter:id|…]]` / `[[lesson:id|…]]`).

`speed-distance` is written (the reference chapter): sd-safe-speed,
sd-limits, sd-stopping, sd-following, sd-energy.

## Traffic law and driving (section "theory")

**road-basics — הדרך ומשתמשיה — rb**
- rb-road: the road and its parts — דרך, כביש, מסלול, נתיב, שול, מדרכה, אי תנועה, שטח הפרדה (בנוי/מסומן), צומת, מחלף, כיכר, מעבר חצייה; דרך עירונית / שאינה עירונית / מהירה (legal definitions, תקנה 1).
- rb-users: road users — נהג/נוהג, הולך רגל (incl. who counts as one: pushing a bike, wheelchair), נוסע, רוכב; duties of each in one line.
- rb-vehicles: vehicle kinds in law — רכב, רכב מנועי, פרטי, מסחרי, משא, ציבורי, מונית, אוטובוס/זעיר, טיולית, טרקטור, טרקטורון, אופנוע/קטנוע/תלת-אופנוע, אופניים, אופניים עם מנוע עזר, גלגינוע, קלנועית, רכב עבודה, מכונה ניידת, גרור, נתמך, רכב ביטחון/הצלה.
- rb-weights: weights and sizes — משקל עצמי, משקל כולל מותר, עומס, why 3,500 kg matters (licence classes, speed, alcohol), vehicle width/length limits (vehicle.* facts).
- rb-law: where the rules come from — פקודת התעבורה, תקנות התעבורה, לוח התמרורים, צווים; who enforces (שוטר, פקח, סדרן); the order of authority: שוטר > רמזור > תמרור > the general rules.

**licensing — רישוי, מסמכים ונהג חדש — lc**
- lc-classes: licence classes and minimum ages (licence.* facts, a table), prerequisites (seniority), what each allows.
- lc-process: the road to a licence — ages to learn and to test, היתר לימוד (טופס ירוק), eye test, the theory test (theory.* facts: questions, time, mistakes, validity), minimum lessons, the practical test.
- lc-new-driver: נהג חדש — duration, accompaniment (day/night months, night hours), the 50-hour accompaniment programme, who may accompany, passenger limit until 21, the „נהג חדש” label, the strict alcohol limit, extension after an offence.
- lc-documents: documents — רישיון נהיגה, רישיון רכב (validity), ביטוח חובה, the periodic vehicle test (vehicletest.* facts), what must be in the car, changing ownership.
- lc-fitness: medical fitness — eyesight and the „משקפיים” condition, medicines and illness, the duty to report, older drivers.

**signs-intro — תמרורים: שפת הדרך — sg** (questions: mostly with `"sign"`)
- sg-overview: how the sign system works — series 100–900 and the appendix symbols, shapes and colours and what each means, supplementary plates, the order of authority, temporary (yellow) signs over permanent ones.
- sg-warning: series 100 — the most important warning signs, grouped (bends, junctions, road surface, people and animals, other hazards); what to do on seeing each.
- sg-mandatory: series 200 (תמרורי הוריה) — direction, lanes, compulsory paths.
- sg-priority: series 300 — 301 give way, 302 stop (full stop at the line), 303 roundabout, and the rest; how priority signs combine with the priority rules.
- sg-prohibition: series 400 — no entry, turns, overtaking, speed (426), weights/heights, stopping/parking (432/433), end-of-restriction signs.
- sg-transit: series 500 — public-transport lanes and stops.
- sg-guide: series 600 — information and direction signs; colour of direction signs; reading an interchange sign.
- sg-roadworks: series 900 — worksite signs, temporary yellow markings, flaggers.
(Light signals (700) and road markings (800) belong to markings-signals.)

**markings-signals — סימוני דרך, רמזורים ואותות — ms**
- ms-lines: longitudinal lines — dashed, continuous, double, continuous+dashed, lane lines, edge lines; what may be crossed.
- ms-transverse: stop line, give-way line / teeth, crossings (pedestrian, bicycle), marked separation area, arrows and words on the road.
- ms-kerbs: kerb colours (park.kerb.* facts, signs 816–819).
- ms-lights: the traffic light (widget traffic-light): the Israeli sequence, flashing green, red+amber, flashing amber, arrows and filter lights, pedestrian and bicycle lights, public-transport lights, lane-control lights (series 700).
- ms-police: police officer's and authorised person's signals; the order of authority; railway-crossing lights and barriers (short; details in special-roads).

**right-of-way — זכות קדימה וצמתים — rw**
- rw-principles: what "זכות קדימה" means (the other may go on without changing speed/direction); give way vs stop; never insist on your right.
- rw-junctions: junctions without signs or lights (the rule of the right, per the regulation), T-junctions, entering from a non-road (driveway, parking, dirt road).
- rw-turning: turning left across oncoming traffic, turning right, U-turns at junctions; pedestrians crossing the road you turn into.
- rw-roundabouts: roundabouts (sign 303) — who has priority, lanes, signalling on exit, large vehicles.
- rw-pedestrians: pedestrian crossings (with and without lights), children, school crossing guards.
- rw-emergency: security/rescue vehicles with lights and siren (following.security_vehicle_gap), a bus leaving a stop, funerals and convoys.
- rw-narrow: narrow roads, an obstacle in your lane, slopes (who gives way uphill/downhill), narrow bridges.

**lanes-overtaking — נתיבים, פניות ועקיפה — lo**
- lo-lanes: keeping right, lane discipline, changing lanes (mirrors, signal, blind spot), lanes at junctions.
- lo-turning: preparing to turn, signalling (how long before), turning manoeuvres, reversing and U-turns — where forbidden.
- lo-overtaking: when overtaking is allowed and how (on the left; exceptions on the right), where forbidden (junction, crossing, bend, crest, continuous line, railway crossing, tunnel, a vehicle that stopped for a crossing, overtaking an overtaker), overtaking cyclists and animals.
- lo-overtaken: duties of the vehicle being overtaken; slow vehicles letting others pass (תקנה 48).
- lo-freeways: freeways (דרך מהירה) — entering with the acceleration lane, merging, leaving, the shoulder, who may not use them, breakdowns there.
- lo-merging: merging and zip-merging, lane drops, heavy traffic, keeping junctions clear.

**stopping-parking — עצירה, העמדה וחנייה — sp**
- sp-definitions: עצירה / העמדה / חנייה — exact differences and why they matter.
- sp-forbidden: where stopping/standing/parking is forbidden (park.no_stop.* facts: junction, crossing, stop line, railway, bridge/tunnel, bus stop, hydrant…), signs 432/433 and kerbs.
- sp-how: parking correctly — parallel parking and the kerb distance (park.curb_distance_max), angle/perpendicular, on a slope (wheels, handbrake, gear), leaving the vehicle safely, opening doors (look back first).
- sp-special: disabled parking (the badge, fines), paid parking, loading zones, parking at night outside cities (lights).
- sp-roadside: stopping on a non-urban road because of a fault — where, hazard lights, the vest, the warning triangle and its distance (equip.* facts).

**special-roads — דרכים ומצבים מיוחדים — sr**
- sr-rail: railway crossings (rail.* facts) — lights, barriers, stopping distance, never stop on the tracks, stuck on the tracks, buses and heavy vehicles.
- sr-tunnels: tunnels and bridges — lights, gaps, no stopping/overtaking, a breakdown or fire in a tunnel.
- sr-transit: public-transport lanes (נת״צ) — who may use them, when; bus stops; taxis.
- sr-roadworks: roadworks — temporary signs and yellow markings over permanent ones, flaggers, speed, lane shifts.
- sr-residential: mixed streets, traffic-calming zones, school zones, bicycle lanes and paths.
- sr-terrain: mountain roads and long descents (low gear, brake fade), dirt and desert roads, flash floods in wadis (never drive into flowing water), dust and sand.

**vulnerable-users — משתמשי דרך פגיעים — vu**
- vu-pedestrians: pedestrians — their rights and duties, crossing behaviour, drivers' duties near crossings, at night.
- vu-children-elderly: children (unpredictable, small, school buses), the elderly, people with disabilities.
- vu-cyclists: bicycles and e-bikes — the rules (ebike.*, bike.* facts: ages, helmets, where they may ride), passing them safely, dooring.
- vu-scooters: electric scooters (escooter.* facts) and light electric vehicles — rules for riders, how drivers should expect them.
- vu-motorcyclists: motorcyclists as seen from a car — visibility, lane filtering, looking twice, gaps.
- vu-animals: animals on the road (livestock, dogs, deer/gazelles, camels in the south) — what to do.

**visibility-weather — תאורה, ראות ומזג אוויר — vw**
- vw-lights: vehicle lights and their use (lights.* facts: dipped-lights season on non-urban roads, main/dipped beam, fog lights, parking lights, hazard lights, motorcycles always on).
- vw-night: night driving — seeing and being seen, glare (oncoming and mirrors), fatigue at night.
- vw-rain: rain and the first rain, aquaplaning (the term has no Hebrew word — glossary id aquaplaning), wipers and demisting, puddles and floods.
- vw-fog-dust: fog, dust, haze and sandstorms (שרב, אובך) — speed, lights, gaps, when to stop.
- vw-heat-cold: heat (tyres, overheating, children in cars), winter in the north and Jerusalem: ice, snow, black ice.
- vw-wind: strong wind — high vehicles, motorcycles, bridges.

**human-factor — הנהג: גורם האדם — hf**
- hf-perception: seeing and attention — the field of view, scanning, inattentional blindness, hazard perception.
- hf-fatigue: fatigue and micro-sleep — signs, what works (stop, sleep) and what does not.
- hf-distraction: distraction — phone rules (phone.* facts, fines), screens, eating, passengers; why hands-free still distracts.
- hf-alcohol: alcohol (alcohol.* facts: both limits, who is under the strict limit, the 3-hour presumption, refusal, penalties) and how alcohol acts on driving; no safe amount; how long it takes to leave the body.
- hf-drugs: drugs and medicines — cannabis, sleeping pills, antihistamines; reading leaflets.
- hf-emotions: emotions, stress, aggression and road rage; young drivers' risk and overconfidence.

**passenger-safety — חגורות, מושבי בטיחות ונוסעים — ps**
- ps-belts: seat belts for everyone (seatbelt.* facts) — how they work, wearing them right, pregnancy, the driver's responsibility.
- ps-children: child restraints (child.* facts: rear-facing, safety seat, booster ages, front seat, airbag off), ISOFIX, installing right.
- ps-forgotten: children left in cars — heat, the legal duties, habits that prevent it, what to do on finding a child in a hot car.
- ps-airbags-restraints: airbags and head restraints — how they protect, distances, child seats and airbags.
- ps-loads-inside: loose objects and pets inside the car; passengers' behaviour; school transport basics.

**defensive-driving — נהיגה נכונה ונהיגה מונעת — dd**
- dd-principles: defensive driving — expect errors of others, keep options open, the space cushion.
- dd-scanning: scanning and mirrors, where to look, the 12-second look ahead, blind spots (widget blind-spots).
- dd-hazards: hazard perception in practice — reading the road, cues (a ball, a door opening, a bus at a stop).
- dd-collisions: avoiding the common crashes — rear-end, head-on (overtaking), side impact at junctions, run-off-road.
- dd-skids: skids and loss of control — under/oversteer basics, what ESC does, recovering (short; technical side in breakdowns).
- dd-courtesy: cooperation and courtesy — signalling intentions, letting others merge, not provoking.

**accidents-emergencies — תאונות ומצבי חירום — ae**
- ae-duties: duties after an accident (accident.* facts: stop, help, call, report within hours, exchange details, insurance) — injury vs damage only; a passenger's duty.
- ae-scene: securing the scene — hazard lights, vest, triangle, where to stand, calling 100/101/102.
- ae-first-aid: basic first aid for drivers — check safety, call מד״א, do not move the injured (except danger), bleeding, breathing; the first-aid kit (equip.first_aid).
- ae-fire-water: fire in or near the vehicle; a vehicle in water or a flood — getting out.
- ae-insurance: reporting, insurance (ביטוח חובה vs comprehensive), photos, witnesses.

**enforcement — עבירות, קנסות ונקודות — en**
- en-offences: kinds of offences — fixed-fine (ברירת קנס) vs court (הזמנה), what happens with each.
- en-fines: common offences with fine and points (a table using {{fine:…}} {{points:…}}): phone, red light, speed bands, seatbelts, children, continuous line, pedestrian crossing…
- en-points: the points system (points.* facts: 12/24/36/72, validity incl. the 2027 change, courses, suspension).
- en-disqualification: disqualification and licence suspension; driving while disqualified; the new driver's special rules.
- en-police: being stopped by the police — what to do, documents, breath tests (refusal = offence).

**eco-driving — נהיגה חסכונית וסביבתית — eco**
- eco-smooth: smooth driving — anticipation, gentle acceleration, coasting, gear choice, cruise control.
- eco-car: the car's part — tyre pressure, load and roof racks, AC and windows, idling, maintenance.
- eco-planning: trip planning, peak hours, car sharing, public transport; EV efficiency (short).
- eco-environment: emissions, noise, the environment; why it also means safety.

## Know your vehicle (section "vehicle")

**vehicle-basics — מבנה הרכב ובדיקות לפני נסיעה — vb**
- vb-systems: the vehicle as systems — engine, drivetrain, brakes, steering, suspension, electrical, body; how they work together (map to the chapters).
- vb-controls: controls and instruments — pedals, gear selector, parking brake, stalks, switches, the instrument cluster.
- vb-position: driving position and mirrors — seat, wheel, head restraint, mirror setting to reduce blind spots.
- vb-checks: checks before driving — a walk-around (tyres, lights, leaks, glass), under the bonnet (fluids), inside (belts, warning lights).
- vb-start: starting, moving off and stopping — manual and automatic, the sequence; switching off and securing.

**engine — המנוע ומערכותיו — eg**
- eg-how: how an engine works — the four-stroke cycle (widget four-stroke), cylinders, crank, valves, timing.
- eg-diesel: petrol vs diesel (widget four-stroke type=diesel) — ignition, glow plugs, turbo, fuel.
- eg-lubrication: lubrication — oil, pump, filter, grades, the dipstick, oil-pressure light ([[dash:oil-pressure]]).
- eg-cooling: cooling — radiator, coolant, thermostat, fan, water pump; overheating ([[dash:coolant-temp]]) and never opening a hot cap.
- eg-fuel-air: fuel and air — tank, pump, injectors, air filter, turbo and intercooler; wrong fuel in the tank.
- eg-exhaust: exhaust and emissions — catalytic converter, lambda, DPF (regeneration), EGR, AdBlue; check-engine light.

**electrical — מערכת החשמל והתאורה — el**
- el-battery: the battery — what it does, life, care, warning signs, safety (acid, hydrogen).
- el-charging: alternator and charging — the battery light ([[dash:battery-charge]]), the belt.
- el-starting: starter and ignition — what happens when you turn the key or press the button; immobiliser.
- el-fuses: fuses and relays — finding and replacing a fuse.
- el-lights: the lights system — kinds of lamps (halogen, xenon, LED), aiming, replacing bulbs, DRL.
- el-jumpstart: jump-starting safely — the order of the clamps, dangers, boosters.

**drivetrain — העברת הכוח — dt**
- dt-clutch: the clutch — how it works, using it, wear and slipping.
- dt-manual: the manual gearbox — gears and ratios, shifting, matching gear to speed and slope.
- dt-automatic: automatics — torque converter, CVT, dual-clutch; P R N D, L/S/manual modes, creeping, never N downhill.
- dt-engine-braking: engine braking and long descents (links to brakes fade and sr-terrain).
- dt-driveline: differential, front/rear/4×4 drive, transfer case; faults and noises.

**brakes — מערכת הבלמים — bk**
- bk-how: hydraulic brakes — pedal, servo, master cylinder, fluid, calipers, discs and drums.
- bk-wear: pads, discs, drums, fluid — wear signs (noise, pulling, vibration), service.
- bk-abs: ABS, EBD, brake assist, emergency braking with ABS.
- bk-parking: parking brake — mechanical and electric, hill-hold.
- bk-fade: brake fade and fluid boiling on long descents; what to do if brakes fail (short; full procedure in breakdowns).
- bk-air: air brakes, spring brakes and retarders in heavy vehicles (licence ["professional"]) — pressure, warning buzzer, draining tanks.

**steering-suspension — היגוי ומתלים — ss**
- ss-steering: steering systems — rack and pinion, hydraulic and electric power steering; heavy steering when the engine is off.
- ss-alignment: wheel alignment — toe, camber, symptoms (pulling, tyre wear).
- ss-suspension: springs, shock absorbers, struts, anti-roll bar; the bounce test; worn shocks and braking distance.
- ss-symptoms: noises, vibration, wandering — what they point to.

**tyres — צמיגים וגלגלים — ty**
- ty-structure: tyre structure and kinds (summer/all-season/winter, run-flat).
- ty-markings: reading the sidewall (widget tyre-code), DOT date and the 6-year rule (tyre.max_age_fit_new), load and speed index.
- ty-pressure: pressure — where to find it, checking cold, under- and over-inflation, TPMS ([[dash:tyre-pressure]]).
- ty-tread: tread depth (tyre.min_tread), wear indicators, uneven wear and what it tells.
- ty-change: changing a wheel step by step (safety first), spare and space-saver limits, repair kits.
- ty-care: rotation, balancing, alignment, same tyres on an axle (tyre.same_axle), buying tyres.

**safety-systems — מערכות בטיחות ועזר לנהג — sy**
- sy-passive: passive safety — body and crumple zones, belts and pretensioners, airbags, head restraints.
- sy-esc: ESC and traction control ([[dash:esc]]) — what they can and cannot do.
- sy-adas: driver assistance — AEB, ACC, lane keeping/departure, blind-spot monitor, parking sensors and cameras, speed-sign reading.
- sy-limits: limits and responsibility — sensors in rain/dirt, over-reliance, the driver is always responsible.

**dashboard — לוח המחוונים ונוריות אזהרה — db**
- db-cluster: the instrument cluster — speedometer, rev counter, fuel, temperature, odometer, trip computer.
- db-colours: warning-light colours and urgency (red/amber/green/blue).
- db-red: the red lights — oil, charging, temperature, brakes, airbag, seatbelt — what to do now.
- db-amber: the amber lights — check engine, ABS, ESC, TPMS, glow plugs, DPF, AdBlue, low fuel.
- db-indicators: green/blue/white indicators — lights, indicators, cruise, eco modes; service reminders.

**maintenance — תחזוקה שוטפת והמבחן השנתי — mt**
- mt-schedule: the service schedule — why, what a service includes, following the handbook.
- mt-diy: the driver's own checks — oil, coolant, brake fluid, washer fluid, tyres, lights, wipers; how to do each safely.
- mt-filters-belts: filters and belts — air, cabin, oil, fuel filters; timing belt/chain and auxiliary belt.
- mt-battery-care: battery care and the 12 V battery in cars that stand long.
- mt-test: the periodic vehicle test (טסט) and the vehicle licence (vehicletest.*, vehiclelicence.* facts) — what is checked, preparing.
- mt-garage: choosing a garage, recalls, genuine vs aftermarket parts, keeping records; seasonal preparation.

**troubleshooting — איתור תקלות — ts** (plus trees, see below)
- ts-method: a method — symptom → when → which system; safety; what a driver may check and what is for a garage.
- ts-noises: noises — squeal, grind, knock, clunk, hum, hiss: what each suggests.
- ts-smoke-smells: smoke colours (white, blue, black) and smells (sweet, burning, fuel, rotten eggs).
- ts-leaks: leaks under the car by colour and place (water from AC is normal).
- ts-vibration: vibration and pulling — at speed, when braking, when turning.
- ts-electrical: electrical faults — nothing works, one thing does not, flickering.
Trees (in `he/trees/<name>.json`, one file each; `car-wont-start` exists): engine-overheating, brake-problems, warning-light-on, strange-noise, smoke-from-car, leak-under-car, car-pulls-to-side, vibration, gear-problems, electrical-fault, engine-power-loss.

**breakdowns — תקלות בדרך ומצבי חירום ברכב — bd**
- bd-procedure: breaking down — getting off the road, hazard lights, vest, triangle (equip.* facts), getting out on the safe side, calling for help.
- bd-tyre-blowout: tyre blowout — hold the wheel, do not brake hard, slow gradually.
- bd-brake-failure: brake failure — pump the pedal, downshift, parking brake gradually, escape lanes and scraping.
- bd-stuck-throttle: stuck accelerator — neutral/clutch, brake hard, stop, ignition off only when stopped.
- bd-steering-lights: steering loss, headlights failure at night, bonnet flies open.
- bd-skid-aqua: skids and aquaplaning — what to do (with dd-skids).
- bd-overheating-fire: overheating in traffic, an engine fire — stop, out, extinguisher only if safe.
- bd-towing: towing and being towed (tow.* facts: bar/rope length, lights, speed), running out of fuel.

**ev-hybrid — רכב חשמלי והיברידי — ev**
- ev-how: how an electric car works — battery, motor, inverter, single-speed drive.
- ev-hybrids: hybrids — mild, full, plug-in; how they switch.
- ev-charging: charging — AC/DC, connectors, home charging safety, public chargers, etiquette.
- ev-driving: driving an EV — instant torque, regenerative braking and one-pedal driving, range and what reduces it, silent at low speed (pedestrians).
- ev-safety: high-voltage safety — orange cables, never open, accidents, fire, flooding, towing an EV.
- ev-maintenance: EV maintenance differences — brakes, tyres, 12 V battery, coolant, battery health.

**motorcycles — אופנועים וקטנועים — mc** (licence ["moto"])
- mc-types: kinds of two-wheelers and licence classes (A2/A1/A), scooters.
- mc-gear: protective gear — helmet (moto.helmet.required), jacket, gloves, boots, visibility.
- mc-controls: controls — throttle, clutch lever, front/rear brakes, gear lever; starting and moving off.
- mc-braking: braking — front and rear, ABS, emergency braking.
- mc-cornering: cornering and counter-steering, lean, road surface.
- mc-traffic: riding in traffic — position in lane, visibility, being seen, what the law says about riding between lanes (check the regulations), passengers (licence.moto.passenger_rule), lights always on.
- mc-maintenance: chain, tyres, brakes — the rider's checks.

**heavy-vehicles — רכב כבד — hv** (licence ["heavy"] unless noted)
- hv-dimensions: weights, axles and dimensions (load.*, vehicle.* facts), overload offences.
- hv-brakes: air brakes, spring brakes, retarder and engine brake — daily checks.
- hv-visibility: blind spots and mirrors (widget blind-spots vehicle=truck), turning and off-tracking, cyclists and pedestrians.
- hv-loads: loading and securing, overhang and wide-load rules (load.*), marking.
- hv-trailers: trailers and semi-trailers — coupling (fifth wheel, kingpin), jackknife, reversing (licence ["heavy"], CE).
- hv-hours: driving hours and rest (hours.* facts), the tachograph and speed limiter (licence ["professional"]).
- hv-tractors: tractors and work vehicles (licence ["tractor"]).

**buses — אוטובוסים ורכב ציבורי — bs** (licence ["bus"])
- bs-passengers: passenger safety and comfort — smooth driving, standing passengers, doors.
- bs-stops: stops and bus bays — pulling in and out, pedestrians around the bus.
- bs-rules: bus-specific rules — speed limits and limiter, lanes, driving hours (short, link hv-hours).
- bs-school: school transport — the child-alarm system, counting children, supervision.
- bs-emergency: emergencies — evacuation, fire extinguisher, first-aid kit.
- bs-taxis: taxis (D1) — rules for taxis and passengers.
