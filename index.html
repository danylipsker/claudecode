Apps

Things built in this repository that run in a browser. No installation, nothing leaves the page.

Study

  • Hyper Physics Physics as a map of connected concepts, in the spirit of HyperPhysics: clear explanations, formulas that solve for any variable, simulations to play with, practice with worked solutions.
  • Hyper Math The mathematics of science the same way: algebra to differential equations, a function plotter, typed-answer exercises, and links into the physics that uses it.
  • Hyper Electronics Practical electronics the same way: circuits to op-amps, power converters and logic, with every simulation solved by a real circuit simulator and shown on schematics and an oscilloscope.
  • Hyper Chemistry General chemistry the same way: atoms and bonds to equilibrium, acids, electrochemistry and organic molecules, with an interactive periodic table, an equation balancer and molecules you can turn in 3-D.
  • Hyper Finances Money without the fear: interest, loans and mortgages, budgeting, investing and the stock exchange, leverage, micro and macroeconomics — with money calculators for loans, savings, retirement and more, in your own currency.
  • Hyper Medicine The human body in health and disease: every organ system, mental health, infection, cancer, medicines, diagnosis and evidence, prevention and first aid — with a clickable body map, an ECG monitor and medical calculators.
  • Hyper Aerodynamics How air flows and what it does to everything that moves through it: the atmosphere, airfoils and wings, drag, shock waves, flight performance, stability, propulsion, rotors and wind energy — with an airfoil lab that exports coordinates for CAD, and flight and gas-dynamics calculators.
  • Hyper Hydraulics Liquids that carry, lift and push: pressure, pipes, pumps, channels and water hammer, and oil hydraulics — pumps, cylinders, valves and working circuits drawn in ISO 1219 symbols — with pipe, pump and fluid-power calculators.
  • Hyper Pneumatics Compressed air at work: gas laws and humidity, compressors, air treatment, cylinders, valves, circuits and sequences, electro-pneumatics, vacuum, sizing and the real cost of air — with pneumatics calculators and an ISO 1219 symbol chart.
  • Hyper Pharmaceutics From molecule to medicine: solubility and stability, how drugs are absorbed and cleared, tablets, liquids, creams, injections and inhaled and advanced medicines, quality and safety — with pharmacy calculators, formulation tools and a pharmacokinetics lab.
  • Hyper Biology Life from molecules to ecosystems: cells, metabolism, DNA and genes, biotechnology, evolution, plants, animals, behaviour, microbes and ecology — with a DNA sequence workbench, genetics calculators and a cell explorer.
  • Hyper Feynman The physics Richard Feynman taught, made visible: least action, symmetry, relativity, light, heat and chance, electromagnetism, quantum behaviour and QED's arrows — every idea drawn and simulated, every math term explained. An independent companion to his lectures.
  • Hyper Motors Every kind of motor, explained for choosing and using one: DC, brushless and pancake, induction and single-phase, synchronous, stepper and servo motors, their drives, encoders, limit switches, starters and VFDs, gearing and sizing, heat, noise and vibration, hydraulic and air motors — with a motor lab, sizing tools, wiring diagrams and drive signals.
  • Hyper Ergonomics Designing for people: body sizes and their spread, the dimensions of furniture, rooms, workplaces, machines and vehicles given as ranges with their reasons and limits, lifting, noise, vibration, heat, cold and light — at home, in the office, the workshop, the military and the field.
  • Hyper Driving Driving theory for Israel, in Hebrew and English: traffic law, all 401 road signs, good driving practice and knowing your vehicle — maintenance, warning lights, troubleshooting and handling faults for cars, motorcycles, trucks and buses — with practice questions and a mock theory test.

Puzzles and games

  • The Puzzle Cabinet Over nine thousand puzzles from three thousand years — matches, coins, tangrams, paper folding and cutting, knots, jugs and scales, river crossings, logic, pencil puzzles, 3D cubes, gears, cards and clocks, compass and straightedge, chess, and the riddles of the Sphinx, Alcuin, Dudeney, Loyd and Carroll — each playable with real tools: move, turn, colour, group, see-through, cut, fold, knot, notes and 3D. Endless drawers make new, solver-checked puzzles on demand.
  • Galaxies Nikoli’s Tentai Show. Puzzles generated on the spot, each with a single solution, and a hint that explains its reasoning.
  • Sokoban Push every crate onto a glowing pad. A library of one and a half million levels — Microban and DeepMind’s Boxoban — with thousands built in to play offline, saved progress, and a home-screen install for phones.
  • Sliding Blocks 1,460 sliding block puzzles in ten stages, easiest first: Gridlock (Rush Hour style), Klotski, Release (odd shapes on odd boards), Order (numbers and colours) and Towers (Towers of Hanoi and Panex). Every par is the proven fewest moves, with hints from a built-in solver, offline play and a home-screen install.
  • Tic Tac Toe Play a friend or the computer, at three levels, as either side.

Drawing and geometry

  • VectorLab A vector graphics editor: shape tools, corner rounding, transforms and boolean corners.
  • CURVES A parametric curve lab: famous and math curves, Fourier series, periodic motion, wave forms and space curves. Drive a curve from its parameters and domain, then trace and fit the result.
  • Affine Transformations Stack matrices, multiply them into a composite, and watch what each step does to the figure.
  • Linear Half Toner Turns a picture into a line halftone where line width carries the tone, then into V-bit cut depths for a CNC router.

Mechanisms

  • Unique Gears Pick any shape and it builds the gear that meshes with it, by rolling and then by envelope. Ten illustrated chapters, three labs, SVG and DXF out.

Electronics

  • 555 Timer Sims Twenty-eight circuits from Forrest Mims’s 555 notebook, each opening live in CircuitJS1 with its sliders and switches working.

CNC

  • G-code Viewer Open or paste a program and inspect the toolpath, coloured by move type, with a run-time estimate.
  • G-code Simulator Run a program against a block of stock in 3D, from orthographic or perspective views.
