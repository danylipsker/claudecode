Apps

Things built in this repository that run in a browser. No installation, nothing leaves the page.

Study

  • Hyper Physics Physics as a map of connected concepts, in the spirit of HyperPhysics: clear explanations, formulas that solve for any variable, simulations to play with, practice with worked solutions.
  • Hyper Math The mathematics of science the same way: algebra to differential equations, a function plotter, typed-answer exercises, and links into the physics that uses it.
  • Hyper Electronics Practical electronics the same way: circuits to op-amps, power converters and logic, with every simulation solved by a real circuit simulator and shown on schematics and an oscilloscope.

Puzzles and games

  • Galaxies Nikoli’s Tentai Show. Puzzles generated on the spot, each with a single solution, and a hint that explains its reasoning.
  • Sokoban Push every crate onto a glowing pad. Level picker, move and push counters, saved progress.
  • Tic Tac Toe Play a friend or the computer, at three levels, as either side.

Drawing and geometry

  • VectorLab A vector graphics editor: shape tools, corner rounding, transforms and boolean corners.
  • CURVES A parametric curve lab. Drive a curve from its parameters and domain, then trace and fit the result.
  • Affine Transformations Stack matrices, multiply them into a composite, and watch what each step does to the figure.
  • Linear Half Toner Turns a picture into a line halftone where line width carries the tone, then into V-bit cut depths for a CNC router.

Mechanisms

  • Unique Gears Pick any shape and it builds the gear that meshes with it, by rolling and then by envelope. Ten illustrated chapters, three labs, SVG and DXF out.

Electronics

  • 555 Timer Sims Twenty-eight circuits from Forrest Mims’s 555 notebook, each opening live in CircuitJS1 with its sliders and switches working.

CNC

  • G-code Viewer Open or paste a program and inspect the toolpath, coloured by move type, with a run-time estimate.
  • G-code Simulator Run a program against a block of stock in 3D, from orthographic or perspective views.
