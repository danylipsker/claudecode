Export

Vector files carry the curve as polylines at full sample resolution, black stroke, tightly cropped. DXF (R12) keeps true world coordinates — 3D polylines for space curves — for CAD/CNC. The flat formats (SVG, PDF, EPS, AI, WMF, EMF, HPGL) export exactly what the current view shows: a space curve in 3D mode exports its screen projection, in 2D mode its xy projection. AI is Illustrator-3-compatible PostScript. PNG captures the canvas as pixels.
