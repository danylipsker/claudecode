## Decisions
- One shared numerical module rather than per-sim code: everything (pages' numbers, sims, lab) agrees, and the tests pin it down. One-sided Jacobi was chosen over Golub–Kahan for its simplicity and because the lab can show its rotations one at a time.
- 2 × 2 convention: V is always a pure rotation; a mirror is folded into Σ's last axis (shown as "U incl. mirror"), so the stage animation passes through a flat ellipse for a reflection. For a singular matrix the completed singular vector is signed so U stays a rotation (no spurious "mirror").
- In the affine lab, editing a factor rebuilds M = U·Σ·Vᵀ from the cells and decomposes again (U, V re-orthogonalised) rather than trying to keep an inconsistent edited factor; the grids are refreshed in place so typing is never interrupted by the live update or the stage animation.
- Formula calculators avoid 90°/45° defaults where the solver would divide by cos = 0; formula symbols such as ‖A‖_F are braced so the calculator can highlight them.
- The affine page stays dependency-free (its own tiny expression engine); its SVD uses symEig of MᵀM, which is fine for 2 × 2 and 3 × 3.
