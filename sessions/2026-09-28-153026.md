## Decisions
- Dany's "COP" was read as power factor (cos φ); the power-factor page explains that COP is a heat-pump term — agreed at the start of the build.
- Ergonomics carries its recommendations as structured `ranges` rather than prose tables, so one tool (the Dimension finder) can gather and filter all 694 of them by setting.
- Body data are representative and rounded (in the spirit of ANSUR II / ISO 7250), labelled as such everywhere; standards' tables are described and cited, not reproduced (ISO 13857 gets three key values, not its table).
- The engine's stepper model was replaced after a writer showed it was √2 too pessimistic and subtracted back-EMF as a magnitude; the new phasor model with a leading current gives datasheet-like curves (NEMA 23 on 24 V ≈ 0.5 N·m at 1000 rpm). One sim that had compensated with a low-Kt motor was moved back to standard NEMA 23 data.
- The induction model gained a deep-bar rotor (R2 rising with rotor frequency) because a single cage could not give the realistic ~2× starting torque; the writer who needed the pull-up dip of design letters A–D wrote a double-cage model in its own sims file.
- The lighting tool follows EN 12464-1's one-step-up rule for older eyes instead of made-up multipliers.
- Writers run 10 at a time when limits bite: 20 parallel Opus agents twice died on HTTP 429 ("weekly limit" / "would exceed your account's rate limit"); each was resumed from its saved file with SendMessage after Dany said "Try again".
- HYPER-DRIVING/ and the `driving` entry in .claude/launch.json were left out of this commit: they belong to Dany's parallel "Driving theory app" session.
