## Decisions

- **V-zero (x2 = −x1)** over (b) independent x1/x2 with a centre distance from the working
  pressure angle, and (c) clamping/hiding the field. Measured at x = 0.3 (z 18/24, m 2,
  45°/45°, Σ 90°): same-x build 58 mm³ of overlap, V-zero 0.069 mm³ (0.02 unshifted). (b) with
  the KHK/Niemann formula (z_v = z/cos³β) gave 0.19 mm³: close, but only an approximation on skewed
  axes (moving a shifts the operating helix angles off Σ), and it needs a second spinner plus
  C# plumbing. It is written up in docs §7.5 in case it is wanted later. (c) was weak because the
  same-x build is wrong for any nonzero x, so clamping means disabling the field.
- My first comparison of (b) was wrong: a 2 mm³ bisection threshold made KHK look 0.24 mm off. A
  fine scan showed the overlap fades gradually with point contact. It was corrected to Dany before the choice was made.
