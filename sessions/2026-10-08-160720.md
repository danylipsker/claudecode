# Session — 2026-10-08

**Branch:** main · **Commit:** pending

## Summary
Dany asked that touching any circle on the Hyper apps' Map view jump to that topic, starting with Hyper Medicine. The map had never opened a topic on a real click or tap: the stage captured the pointer on press, so the browser retargeted the release and click to the stage and the circle click handler never fired (the same capture also swallowed the zoom buttons). Fixed once in the shared HYPER-CORE map, which all 17 study apps load; verified in Medicine, Physics, ESP32 and Math.

## What changed
- Map taps and clicks resolve the circle at pointerdown and open the topic on pointerup when there was no drag or pinch; the old `click` handler on the svg is gone (HYPER-CORE/js/ui/map.js)
- Nearest-circle hit test within 16 screen px, so a finger lands the 2 px outer concept dots (same file)
- Drag threshold raised from 1 px of movement to 6 px of displacement from the press, with a catch-up so the pan does not jump; a drag that starts on a circle pans and never navigates
- Zoom buttons (+, −, fit) no longer take part in the pointer capture, so their clicks reach them again
- `setPointerCapture` guarded with try/catch (a synthetic pointer id throws)

## Decisions
- Fixed in HYPER-CORE rather than per app: every Hyper study app loads the same map.js, so one change covers all of them. Hyper Driving is a separate app with no topic map (its circles are sign art) and was left alone.
- Tap resolved on pointerup rather than through `click`, because with pointer capture Chrome retargets click to the capturing element; releasing capture before click would break the drag.
- Hit tolerance is in screen pixels (max of the circle's drawn radius and 16 px) so it is finger-sized at every zoom level; a tap in empty space does nothing.
