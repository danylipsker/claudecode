## Summary

Dany asked for every sign of the Ministry sign chart (`HYPER-DRIVING/luach-tamrurim-2021.pdf`) as
an SVG file: extracted, named by its original number, traced with the shapes and colours kept, and
saved in a folder named DRIVING-SIGNS. The result is `HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/`: 436 SVG files
for 391 sign numbers, a gallery page, an index and the scripts that made them. It is committed and
pushed.

## What changed

- 436 traced SVG files, one per picture in the chart, named after the printed number: `101.svg`,
  `127פ.svg`, `ס-20.svg`, and `128_1.svg` / `128_2.svg` where one number shows several pictures
  (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/*.svg)
- A gallery with search by number or name and three stages (white, checker, dark) to judge the
  transparency (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/index.html)
- An index with number, chart page, Hebrew and English name, size, colours, background and trace
  scale per file (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/index.csv)
- The extraction script: reads the table rows from the page geometry, names each picture after
  the number of its row (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/_tools/build_signs.py)
- The tracer: palette, unmixing, halo removal, background, colour layers
  (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/_tools/signtrace.py)
- The outliner: smooth curves with sharp corners from a bitmap mask
  (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/_tools/smoothtrace.py)
- Notes on naming, limits and rebuilding (HYPER-DRIVING/DRIVING-SIGNS/README.md)

## Decisions

- **The files are traces, because the PDF holds no vectors.** The chart is a Word document; its
  signs are raster pictures 60 - 300 px wide, most of them JPEG. Extracting "the vector" was not
  possible, so the work went into the tracer.
- **The folder sits beside the PDF**, in HYPER-DRIVING, not at the repo root. Dany named the
  folder but not its place; the signs belong to the driving app.
- **Names are the chart's own**, Hebrew letters included (`127פ`, `ס-20`). The chart prints the
  symbols with a hyphen (ס-20); `content/il/signs.json` writes them without (ס20). The files follow
  the chart, the titles inside the files come from signs.json.
- **A number with several pictures gets `_1`, `_2` …** in reading order (top to bottom, right to
  left), rather than one file holding all the pictures, so each file is one sign.
- **Own tracer instead of potrace or vtracer.** Plain potrace gave colour fringes along
  anti-aliased edges, collapsed strokes 1 - 2 px wide and drew frames with uneven thickness.
  vtracer was installed and not used. Only potrace's path finder is kept (`potracer`, pure
  Python); colour separation, smoothing and curve fitting are in the two scripts.
- **Colours are the measured ones**, not repainted from a standard palette, because the request
  was to keep the chart's colours. The app's own sign renderer (js/signs.js) uses a palette; the
  two are separate on purpose.
- **Transparent ground around a sign, white kept inside it.** 384 files are transparent around
  the sign; 37 keep an opaque white ground (signal housings, striped boards, road pictures). The
  rule is automatic with three short exception lists at the top of build_signs.py: `KEEP_WHITE`,
  `HOLLOW`, `DOTTED`.
- **Hard-edged and anti-aliased pictures are traced differently.** The chart mixes both kinds.
  Anti-aliased ones are upsampled 3x from the sub-pixel coverage; hard-edged ones are outlined at
  their own resolution with a wider smoothing band.
- **Committed straight to main**, as this repo always has been.
- **The PDF stays out of git.** It is gitignored as third-party reference. I pointed out that
  the traced files derive from it before committing; Dany then asked to commit and push.

## Open threads

- **The app does not use these files.** Hyper Driving still draws its signs from data
  (`content/il/signs.json`, js/signs.js). Whether the app should show the traced chart pictures
  anywhere was not discussed.
- **Quality limits that come from the source**, listed in the README: small lettering on the
  direction signs is rough; the message boards 933 and 934 are approximate; the dotted lamp
  circles of 720 and 721 are uneven; long arcs are slightly uneven at very high zoom. A cleaner
  result for a given sign needs a better source picture or redrawing by hand.
- **24 symbol rows have no picture in the chart** (ס-1 to ס-3, ס-18, ס-19, ס-76 to ס-79, ס-94 to
  ס-99, ס-121 to ס-129), so they have no file.
- **Signs newer than the 2021 chart are missing** (for example 153, 638 - 640, ס-140, ס-141, which
  signs.json has). They need another source.
- **The folder is served with the rest of the site** if GitHub Pages publishes this repo, so the
  traced chart pictures are public there. Not checked in this session.
- **A full rebuild takes about 5 minutes** and needs `pymupdf`, `numpy`, `scipy`, `pillow` and
  `potracer` in the Python on PATH. `vtracer` is installed and can be removed.
- Git will convert the SVG line endings from LF to CRLF in the working copy the next time it
  touches the files (autocrlf). The committed content is LF.
