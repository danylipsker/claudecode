became family 27: the pair makers call Fibonacci or Nautilus gears. Two identical non-circular
gears on golden-spiral pitch curves; one turned steadily drives the other faster and slower,
1 : 1 on average, the speed jumping back at every step. It is in the app and the Task Pane

## Decisions
- **"Fibonacci gears" = the Nautilus pair.** Chosen from what the term means online
  (Thingiverse/Printables/MakerWorld). Not a gear train with Fibonacci tooth counts. Dany did
  not confirm the reading; the final report asked him to say if he meant the other.
- **The real spiral, not the quarter arcs.** The pitch curve is the true log spiral, not the
  Fibonacci quarter-circle approximation: arcs would put a curvature jump in the pitch curve
  at every joint.
- **Identical gears by a half-integer lobe.** Each lobe is z_L + 1/2 pitches, so a lobe carries
  z_L whole teeth, a half tooth at the big end and a half gap at the small end. One part to
  make, printed or cut twice.
- **The step needed a relief.** The pitch curves hand over exactly, but the ratio jumps from g
  to 1/g. Rack-cut teeth then collide there: up to 2.6 mm² just before a step, 0.57 mm² over
  40° after it. By a pi-rotation / time-reversal symmetry, one window clears both sides.
  - **Window after the step chosen.** It takes the big corner's half tooth, one tip and a
    0.5 mm scoop of the step face (4.6 mm²). The window before the step would have ground the
    small end's first three teeth to stubs (7.6 mm²).
  - **Cost.** Gear 2 can lag the driver up to 3.4° within ±0.5° of a step; none from 25°.
  - **Speed.** The window comes from the corner kinematics, not from scanning overlaps: on
    20 µm outlines, flank contact looked like overlap and the scan ran to 85°. The sweep is
    sampled by distance from the pitch point. Shapely `distance()` costs 240 ms and must stay
    out of loops. The relief takes 1.2 s.
- **Defaults.** One golden lobe, 55 teeth, module 1, 3 mm bores: 55 is the smallest Fibonacci
  tooth count whose small end does not undercut at 20°.
- **Profile shift hidden for this card.** One standard rack must cut both gears so their tooth
  phases match across the steps.
- **Commit only this session's work.** The "Fix backlash thickening involute teeth" session was
  staging in the same repo at the same time (one shared git index). b39634a holds only this
  session's 17 files: a PROGRESS.md and a docs/gear-math.md built as HEAD + Family 27 / §29,
  with the other session's four code files unstaged first. That session was told the push was
  done and commits its own work next.
