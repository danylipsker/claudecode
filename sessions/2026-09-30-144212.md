## Decisions

- **More time, not smaller boards, for hard LITS.** A 10×10 LITS attempt takes ~1 s and only about
  one in four comes out unique; mixing in 9×9 boards did not help because they grade as level 3 and
  level 5 rejects them. With 12 s every tried seed produced a puzzle in the browser (1–6 s).
- **Making the drawer async was required by that**: a synchronous 12 s search would freeze the tab.
- **Stopped means stopped.** On "ENOUGH" all writers were halted at once and none were resumed. The
  writers had already regenerated data/catalog.js with 10 half-built families (311 unchecked
  puzzles, 2 validate errors): catalog.js was restored from git and the 26 partial files were moved
  to the session scratchpad (`unfinished-wave2/`), not deleted and not committed.
- Wave 2 used Sonnet writers, six at a time, because the weekly all-models limit was at 67–69%.
