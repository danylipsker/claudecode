## Decisions
- **Atlas is a redraw, not a facsimile**: circuit topologies/values/data tables are reproduced as
  facts, but all schematics are drawn fresh and all prose rewritten — deliberately avoiding a
  page-by-page copy of the copyrighted book's artwork and text. Credited to Mims on the page.
- **Sims favor demonstrable behavior over literal part lists** where CircuitJS1 lacks elements:
  piezo buzzers → resistive loads or gated audio astables (p.21), photoresistors/phototransistors →
  sliders and beam switches, relays → L+R coil with real flyback diode, 556 → two 555 elements.
  Each adaptation is labeled on the canvas. p.29 was later made fully faithful (Q2 restored).
- **Sliders instead of PotElm** for all pots — slider `38` line format was verified; PotElm's
  3-terminal geometry wasn't worth the layout risk.
- **Per-circuit timesteps**: slow timers use 5e-5/2e-5 s so demos don't crawl; audio stays 5e-6.
- **Shareable links use `cct=` (raw URL-encoded text)**, verified working in the current falstad
  build; `ctz=` compression was skipped (would require reimplementing the app's LZW).
