## Summary
A short session on the apps landing page (`index.html` at the repo root). Dany asked for the Hyper family
of apps to be arranged together, in alphabetical order, keeping the same appearance. All 17 Hyper cards now
sit in the **Study** list, sorted by name; the page looks exactly as before apart from the order.

## What changed
- The 17 Hyper cards are gathered under **Study** and sorted alphabetically: Aerodynamics, Biology,
  Chemistry, Driving, Electronics, Ergonomics, Feynman, Finances, Hydraulics, Math, Medicine, Motors,
  Optics, Pharmaceutics, Physics, Pneumatics, Projections (`index.html`).
- Hyper Optics and Hyper Projections moved out of **Drawing and geometry** to join the rest of the family.
  That section keeps VectorLab, Curves Workshop, CURVES, Affine Transformations and Linear Half Toner.

## Decisions
- **Only the order changed.** Each card was moved as a whole block, so every dot colour, description and
  link is byte-for-byte what it was. The reorder was done by a throwaway script that checked the file has
  the same set of lines and the same length before and after, and kept the CRLF line endings.
- **Optics and Projections went to Study rather than staying in Drawing and geometry.** "Together" was read
  as one group for the whole family; splitting the family across two headings would not have met the
  request.
- **Card text was left alone, including wording that now reads oddly** (see Open threads). The request
  said to keep the same appearance, so rewording was not done without being asked.
- Checked in the browser (`apps` preview on port 8172): the six headings list the expected cards in the
  expected order, each with its own dot class, and the console is clean.

## Open threads
- Three descriptions say "the same way" — Hyper Math ("The mathematics of science the same way"), Hyper
  Electronics and Hyper Chemistry. The phrase pointed back to Hyper Physics when that was the first card.
  Physics is now 15th, so the phrase has nothing before it to refer to. Dany was told and has not asked
  for a change; rewording those three is a small follow-up if wanted.
- Hyper Optics, Hyper Physics and Hyper Projections all use the default blue dot. Not new (it was so
  before the move), but they now sit close together in one list; a distinct dot colour for Optics and
  Projections would be a small follow-up if wanted.
