## Summary
Dany asked for a pitch circle on every gear the GEARS GENERATOR makes: a circle in the
central plane, a 1 mm surface at the mid-face, a checkbox to leave it out, metric and
inch alike, shown in the main view with a show/hide, in a colour that pops. Delivered
end to end across the Python engine, the STEP export, the WPF viewer, the standalone
SolidWorks exporter and the Task Pane add-in, verified in SolidWorks 2020 and 2026.

## What changed
- One pitch marker per toothed body for all 25 gear types, in the global frame the
  solids are built in (py/gear_step/pitch_circle.py): a circle (centre, axis, diameter)
  or, for racks, belts, chain links and the Fibonacci gears, a pitch line / pitch
  spiral. Bevel-type members get the pitch-cone circle at the mean cone distance on
  their own axis. docs/pitch-circle.md tabulates where each circle sits.
- The server answers `outline` and `export_step` with `pitch_markers`; the STEP carries
  a 1 mm band of surface plus the circle's edge per body (py/gear_step/server.py,
  build_gear.py `pitch_circles` context). `pitch_circle: false` leaves them out.
- SolidWorks: the band comes in as a surface body, named after its circle and coloured
  orange; the circle is drawn as a named orange 3D sketch of two three-point arcs
  (src/GearGen.SolidWorks/PitchCircleFeatures.cs, linked into the app and the add-in).
  The exporter reopens the part when SolidWorks 2020 imported the STEP as an assembly.
- The panel gained a PITCH CIRCLE section (create / show boxes and a summary of the
  diameters); the 3D view draws the circle and a translucent band in orange
  (GearGen.UI/PitchCircleVisuals.cs, GearPanel.xaml, GearViewModel.cs).
- `--swtest` reads a `<step>.pitch.json` sidecar; `--swopen` now lists surface bodies,
  their colours and the sketches with each arc's centre and radius.
- 17 new engine tests (py/gear_step/tests/test_pitch_circle.py); README and PROGRESS.

## Decisions
- The circle is drawn through the SolidWorks API, not read from the STEP: measured on a
  prototype, SolidWorks 2020 opens a free face as a surface body but ignores a free
  edge. Two three-point arcs were chosen over CreateCircleByRadius because the arc's
  plane follows its points, so a bevel mate's or screw gear's tilted circle needs no
  reference plane.
- The markers are computed from the families' params objects, not from the built
  solids, so the preview (an STL) and the STEP share them without re-tessellation; the
  viewer builds its own line and strip geometry, which makes show/hide instant.
- "Add the pitch circle" is a parameter (GearParameters.PitchCircle, sent to the
  engine); "Show in the 3D view" is a view setting only. The two are independent.
- The 1 mm band is 1 mm in both unit systems (Dany said 1 mm); markers stay in mm and
  the panel converts the summary to inches.
- The worm's preview wheel and the face gear's preview pinion are `preview_only`:
  drawn in the view, kept out of the STEP, matching the existing export convention.
- The timing wheel's pitch circle is its outside diameter (the belt module keeps the
  pitch line on the OD), so its test checks tangency instead of intersection.
- Four Explore subagents mapped the 25 families' frames in parallel; their reports
  were the basis of pitch_circle.py and were cross-checked by the intersection tests.
