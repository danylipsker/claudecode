## Decisions

- **Commit the generator, not just its output.** The previous session's top open
  thread was that circuit 24 regressed five times because fixes were applied to
  generated files while the generator stayed in a temp scratchpad. Both regressions
  found today are the same failure mode. The `.txt` files are build artifacts; leaving
  their source outside the repo guarantees the next regeneration undoes any fix.
- **Timing networks are drawn to the left of every 555.** CircuitJS puts pins 7, 2 and
  6 on the chip's left face. Mims draws them on the right on several pages, and
  following his placement ran the connecting wires straight across the chip body.
  Same parts, same connections, mirrored placement — topology confirmed unchanged by
  diffing circuit 06's netlist against page 10 afterwards.
- **Potentiometers keep their diagonal `x` attribute.** It looks like an unaligned
  element but `PotElm` derives the wiper position from that minor-axis delta;
  straightening it puts the wiper on the track and breaks the part. The linter
  exempts `pt` for this reason.
- **A wire ending part-way along another wire is a defect, not a style question.**
  CircuitJS joins elements only where posts share coordinates, so such a join *looks*
  connected and is not. `lint.py` flags it as T-JOINT alongside overlaps and
  wires-across-chip-bodies; it found 124 issues across 13 circuits, now zero.
- **Probes stay.** The book draws output arrows and CircuitJS needs an element to
  attach a scope to. A voltmeter probe is high-impedance and changes nothing
  electrically, so it is the cheapest honest stand-in — but each one now gets its own
  vertical lane rather than cutting diagonally across the schematic.
