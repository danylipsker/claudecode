both single-file, dependency-free HTML in the house dark theme. First the **G-code
viewer** (`GCODE-VIEWER/gcode-viewer.html`): parse-and-inspect tool scoped to what the

  G17/18/19 arc planes, block delete, bare-T tool changes, G41/42/G43 warnings);
  heightmap stock carving with exact flat/ball/V tip profiles and 10%-interval
  snapshots so scrubbing backward restores material; R32F height texture displaced
  in the vertex shader; revolved tool mesh with shank + holder, static at scrub
  position and animated during playback; per-tool colored paths (executed bright /
  pending dim); 2D locked-top and full-orbit 3D with seamless ortho/perspective and
  Iso/Top/Front/Right/Left/Back presets; auto stock from toolpath limits (editable);

## Decisions

- **Viewer v1 targets house output exactly** (halftoner dialects incl. G2/G3 arc
  fitting and modal words); arbitrary-file robustness was the deliberate second
  step — then largely absorbed into the simulator's parser anyway.
- **NOVELTECH A/B/C G-code stays out of both apps.** Its words are joint angles that
  need forward kinematics + machine parameters; both apps warn explicitly instead of
  rendering nonsense. A future FK import mode is the right shape for it.
- **Hand-rolled WebGL2, no three.js** — repo has no vendored 3D lib and the house
  pattern is self-contained files; a heightmap displaced in the vertex shader from
  an R32F texture makes the carve cheap (CPU stamps min() into a Float32Array, GPU
  re-renders). 2.5D heightmap accepted over true voxels: undercuts can't exist in
  3-axis milling, so nothing of value is lost.
- **Idempotent min() stamping + snapshots** rather than an undo log: partial-segment
  re-stamping is harmless, rewind restores the nearest ≤10% snapshot and re-carves
  forward within a 24 ms/frame budget ("simulating…" chip when behind).
- **G18 arcs follow the LinuxCNC (Z,X) axis-order convention** (the "reversed-looking"
  standard behavior), verified against the known gotcha rather than naive intuition.
- **Rapid-crash tolerance is profile-aware** (V/ball surface sits above the tip by
  d·cot(α/2) at the snapped cell) and skips the segment start — retracting out of
  your own cut is not a crash. This killed 10 false positives in the demo.
- Bugs found by actually driving the apps in the browser, worth remembering as a
  class: GLSL precision mismatch (`ivec2` uniform highp-vs-mediump across stages)
  silently killing the whole script via TDZ; object-spread field collision
  (`{t: time, ...c}` where c.t was the tool number) breaking tool-change pauses;
  lowp default sampler precision on R32F heights.
