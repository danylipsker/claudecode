## What changed
- Four library groups between "Space curves — 3D" and "My curves", driven by a `GROUPS` table instead of hard-coded group lines (`CURVES/CURVES.html`): Fourier series (12), Periodic motion (12), Math curves (23), Wave forms (21).
- Expression prelude helpers so the curves show their real formulas in the equation box: `fsum(a,b,k=>…)` series sum, `fint(a,b,u=>…,n)` Simpson integral, `kepler(M,e)` Newton solve of Kepler's equation, plus `frac`, `clamp`, `step`, `sinc`. The editor help text documents them with examples and mentions ternary conditions.
- `hasZ(c)` replaces the two ad-hoc "has a z component" checks (3D tag, auto 2D/3D switch), so non-custom groups without z stay 2D.
- Library filter also matches group titles ("wave", "fourier").
- Header subtitle, script banner, the app index card (`index.html`) and the README row describe the new families.

## Decisions
- Fourier curves use explicit partial sums via `fsum` with a harmonic-count slider N, rather than opaque named helpers, so the displayed `y(t)` is the textbook series. The epicycle polygon uses the closed form c_k ∝ 1/k² for k ≡ q (mod m), which also yields star polygons (m=5, q=2 pentagram) from one entry.
- Wave forms are closed-form (sign/asin/frac/clamp) rather than Fourier sums — the two groups are deliberately complementary; "Ringing" and "Damped oscillation" overlap on purpose, framed with τ versus γ.
- Pendulum phase curve is drawn as an energy contour (±√ branches via the t-doubling trick already used by the Cassinian ovals) because the time-domain large-amplitude pendulum needs elliptic functions the expression language lacks.
- Bouncing ball with restitution computes the bounce index with floor/log in the aux helpers; the rest state after the finite bounce time returns 0 explicitly.
- Time plots keep the app's uniform-scale fit; domains were chosen for roughly 3:1 to 5:1 aspect instead of adding a stretch-to-fit view option, to leave the UX unchanged.
- Maurer rose and Spiral of Theodorus rely on the sample count equalling the point count (the polyline through the samples is the figure); notes say so.
- Left untouched: an untracked `CURVES-BOOK/` folder and uncommitted edits to `.claude/launch.json` and `.gitignore` that another concurrent session was writing during this one (a 300 MB scanned Yates handbook PDF among them). Not staged here on purpose.
