## Decisions
- **Fillet centre on the tooth-space side** — the textbook construction. The inverted
  version had passed validity, simplicity, crossing, manifoldness and "matches my hand
  calculation" checks because it was a valid polygon of the wrong shape; only a
  point-in-polygon material probe told the two apart. Rule adopted: a silhouette-only
  artifact is geometry until a material probe says otherwise; verify fillets against an
  external formula, not the construction's own variables.
- **3D Interconnect off during import**, rather than asking users to change SolidWorks
  options: it was the cause of both the template-dialog stall and the linked (non-native)
  parts. Forcing default templates was tried first and measured NOT to be the cause.
- **Multi-member families are verified by boolean intersection**: (near) zero for the
  correct placement, a clear collision for the same members turned half a pitch — so each
  test is known to be able to fail. Before writing any phase logic, the tooth/space frame
  on +Y is *measured* for each member's outline (sun: tooth; internal ring: space).
- **Herringbone**: `hand` is the half at z=0; the gap is a root-diameter cylinder; the upper
  half starts pre-rotated and twists back to zero so both end faces are un-rotated.
- **Helical rack is a variant of the Rack family**, not a new family (as Spur/Helical share
  the cylindrical family). Its hand is derived from `involute.py`'s twist sign — a rack is a
  gear of infinite radius with its teeth on top — so a right-hand rack meshes with a
  left-hand pinion; the test derives the sign rather than restating it.
- **Screw pair**: `β2 = Σ − β1` (negative ⇒ opposite hand, zero ⇒ spur gear 2); gear 2 is
  rotated about the centre-distance line by `−Σ` for a right-hand gear 1, derived from the
  two tooth traces having to coincide; STEP exports both members as a two-body part.
- **Planetary**: spur members only (the internal gear has no helix); the ring is generated
  with the planet as its cutter; the card auto-picks a planet tooth count that satisfies
  the assembly condition; for an even planet the *sun* turns half a pitch.
- **Cycloidal gear**: one rolling circle for both curves (a pair's conjugacy = a shared
  circle), default `r_g = R/2` (radial flanks), no root fillet; pressure angle hidden.
- **Cycloidal drive**: profile derived from the kinematics as an inward offset (not the
  usual copied closed form); the curvature guard considers convex stretches only (an
  inward offset never self-intersects in the concave valleys — the default disc has
  1.2 mm valleys and a perfectly valid profile); output holes are `d + 2E`.
- **Commit discipline**: only `GEARS GENERATOR` files were staged all day. `CIRCUITS/*` and
  `.vs/*` changes and the untracked `wpfCalculator/` (a nested repo) belong to other
  sessions and were left as found.
