## Decisions
- **Legal numbers that stay typed, on purpose:** offence bands (speeding by 21–30 km/h), values a sign itself defines, historical dates, worked examples, technical codes. They are part of an offence's or sign's identity, not a value the Ministry changes on its own. Written into AUTHORING.md §4 and §9.
- **Physics never follows a legal fact.** The "2-second glance covers 28 m" example states 50 km/h directly. It had used the urban speed-limit fact, so a change of law would have broken the arithmetic.
- **Scenario numbers stay typed, with their threshold listed.** A question about "a 14,000 kg truck" lists the 10,000 kg fact in its `facts`, so the editor shows the question under that fact after a law change.
- **Signs are redrawn, never copied.** No official picture goes into the app (no bitmaps, no `svg` overrides). Everything stays shapes and paths, so the Ministry can still change a sign through a content update.
- **The booklet and the poster stay out of the repository.** Both are the Ministry's or a third party's material: `luach-tamrurim-2021.pdf` and `86913_ykq6.png` are gitignored, as is the whole `_sheets/` reference folder.
- **Where the chart and the app disagreed, the chart won.** That covers the 504, 438, ס120 and ס4 corrections above, after reading the booklet's own text for each row.
- **Fuel-brand symbols (ס36–ס39) are bold wordmarks,** not the companies' logo artwork.
- **Sonnet for the nine sign writers,** one per series, each with its own work file and glyph prefix. I reviewed every series' comparison sheet before merging.
