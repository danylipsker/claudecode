## Summary

Dany reported, with measurements, that the involute generator's backlash had the wrong sign in
GEARS GENERATOR: z=20 m=2 grew from 1238.04 to 1280.73 mm² of section with 0.5 mm of backlash.
Confirmed, fixed, tested, committed and pushed (`f2c61aa`), then checked in SolidWorks 2026: the
same gear with 0.5 mm backlash now imports 442 mm³ lighter than without (11,937.3 vs 12,379.3 mm³,
equal to the engine's own numbers). A positive profile shift had been thinning the tooth too; that is fixed as well.

## What changed

All in GEARS GENERATOR, commit `f2c61aa`:

- The generating rack's cutter tooth is now `p - s` wide on the pitch line (it cuts the gear's
  space), built as the basic rack: `pi m_t/4 + b/2` half-width on its datum line, the datum moved
  out by `x m_n`. It was `s` wide, which only equals `p - s` at x = 0 with no backlash, so backlash
  thickened teeth and a positive shift thinned them, both by the full amount (py/gear_step/involute.py)
- `circular_tooth_thickness`'s shift term uses the normal module (ISO 21771,
  `m_t (pi/2 + 2 x tan alpha_n)`); it overstated a helical gear's by 1/cos(beta), 0.08 mm at m=2,
  x=0.3, beta=30 deg (py/gear_step/involute.py)
- The internal ring's shaper cutter now gets the backlash negated: its teeth are the ring's
  spaces. Rings had been right only because the two sign errors cancelled; they measure the
  same as before (py/gear_step/internal.py)
- Regression tests: pitch-circle thickness vs `circular_tooth_thickness` for backlash 0/0.2/0.5,
  x = 0/±0.3, spur and helical, plus the involute thickness halfway to the tip; area loss from
  backlash; the cutter's own width, widening and rigid shift; the ISO formula; the ring's tooth
  thickness. 13 of the new tests fail on the old code; the full suite passed, 271 tests in 19 min
  (py/gear_step/tests/test_involute.py, test_internal.py)
- gear-math §4.2 (the cutter), §7.2 (helical `s`), §11.2 (the shaper's backlash), §13 check 3;
  a PROGRESS entry (docs/gear-math.md, PROGRESS.md)

## Decisions

- The cutter is defined from the basic rack, not as `(p - s)/2` from `circular_tooth_thickness`,
  so the pitch-circle test checks the formula against independent geometry instead of against
  itself.
- `fibonacci_gear.py`, `rack.py` (the rack's own tooth) and `cycloidal.py` were left alone: each
  has its own, already correct, thickness. Bevel, spiral bevel, hypoid, face, worm, globoid,
  hyperboloidal and strain-wave flexsplines never pass a backlash or shift, so they are unchanged.
  No C# change: `GearDerivedValues.QuickEstimate` is only the pre-engine fallback.
- The fix was made in a copy inside the worktree (Edit/Write refuse the nested repo from a
  worktree session), delivered as a patch plus sha256 manifest, and applied with `git apply` only
  after Dany said yes.
- The commit holds only this fix's hunks: PROGRESS.md and gear-math.md also held the Fibonacci
  session's uncommitted work. Both sessions share the nested repo's one git index; a guard on the
  staged file list stopped a mixed commit. By message, the Fibonacci session committed first
  (`b39634a`), then this fix was re-staged on top (`f2c61aa`).
- No add-in rebuild: the fix is Python, and the add-in runs the repo's `py/gear_step/server.py`
  from its bin folder. The DLLs were already built from current sources at 09:49, and the "Move
  add-in library out of OneDrive" session was mid-work in the add-in.
- The SolidWorks check used the add-in's own creation path (engine STEP, then import) via the app's
  `--swtest ... 2026` and volumes read over COM, not the Task Pane: the add-in self-test has no
  backlash key, and its trigger file belonged to another session (stale by then).
