## Decisions
- Separate apps per discipline on one engine, as before; Pharmaceutics loads medtools.js too (clinical PK
  functions live in medicine.js and are reused, not duplicated).
- Safety framing: all drugs in examples, calculators and sims are hypothetical; "explain, never prescribe";
  generic names only; no recipes for harm or for defeating abuse deterrents; emergency callouts on overdose,
  toxicity and CNS pages. Biology pages explain science without lab protocols or dual-use detail.
- Engine gaps writers reported were fixed centrally when they affected correctness everywhere (solver ends,
  symbol keys, binomial bias, NCA λz); convenience helpers they wrote inside their own sims files
  (t distribution, normal CDF, acceptance value, lung deposition, Farquhar photosynthesis) were left there.
- Bioequivalence: kept the paired analysis as the default when no sequence is given, and added the proper
  2×2 crossover (n − 2 df) when each subject's sequence is supplied, rather than silently changing df.
- Tool links were added as one line in each page's applications list (a small script), not by rewriting
  writers' bodies.
- Index files are CRLF; the wiring script keeps line endings instead of mixing them.
