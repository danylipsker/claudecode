## Summary
Two strain wave gear families joined the GEARS GENERATOR: the Harmonic Drive cup unit of
harmonicdrive.net (wave generator, flexspline, circular spline) and the nested multi-stage
drive of the driving 4 answers video (2:00-20:00), where every outer wave generator is a
ring whose internal teeth are the stage inside it. Both build on exact geometry: the
flexspline is bent as an inextensible ring and the rings' teeth are generated as the
envelope of the bent teeth, so the mesh cannot interfere and the contact is measured.
The cards carry the ratio, a target-ratio recommender, bearing sizes, a material table
and the output speed. Then two rounds of polish from Dany's tries: the nested preview
went from 25 s to 1.4 s, and the cards' tables were re-laid for their column with a
resizable, foldable 3D view.

## Decisions
- **Rings are generated, not assumed.** The circular spline's spaces are the envelope of
  the flexspline tooth over a lobe passage; the S-tooth of the commercial units is not
  modelled. A generating tooth lengthened by the root clearance was tried first and left
  12 µm of flank play at 40 teeth (0 % contact); the true tooth generates and only the
  space floor is stretched for the clearance.
- **Zero backlash by default, 2 µm numerical buffer**, contact reported as "teeth within
  backlash + 0.01 m": 29 % of 200 teeth on the default unit, the catalogue's "up to 30 %".
- **Export polylines biased inward by the tolerance** (each member shrunk by tol before
  simplifying) so touching members never cross; the bearing race and cam share the
  band's bore polygon vertex for vertex. Rejected: loosening the interpenetration
  thresholds, and B-spline flanks (the envelope's micron scallops read as corners and
  made 22 000 faces).
- **The preview does not build OpenCASCADE solids.** Every member is a prism of its
  section (the cup wall a ruled band), so the STL is written from the polygons; the
  STEP still builds solids, with the housing sewn from shared-edge faces (a glue fuse
  was slower than the plain fuse, sewing 4× faster).
- **Thread pool, not processes, for the envelopes**: shapely releases the GIL, 3× on
  eight rings, and no orphaned workers when the app kills the engine.
- **Default cup unit 200/202 at module 0.413** so the standard 812 (60×80×13) bearing
  seats it; the nested default is the video's 40/68/96/124 at module 1, bearingless.
- **Materials**: the first passing material in preference order is recommended, not the
  highest safety factor (that always named titanium and TPU); TPU is listed as not for
  teeth. Figures are typical handbook values, flagged on the card.
- **The tables stay in the parameter column**, re-laid as two-line rows for its 276 px,
  rather than moving to the wide details area — consistent with the compound planetary
  card, and the details area now competes with the 3D view for height.
- Build from PowerShell: the Bash sandbox cannot see `C:\Program Files`, so the interop
  references fail there and look like code errors.
