## Decisions
- Family previews in the add-in are pre-rasterized PNGs at catalog-build time, not an SVG library in the
  C# project: no new NuGet dependency, and the render pipeline (headless Chrome) was already proven for QA.
  One Chrome launch for all families (grid page + Pillow crops) instead of one per family.
- TR categories that are not parametric fasteners (enclosure locks/latches/gaskets, cable management
  beyond ties/clips, tooling, castors, medical) were left out deliberately and are listed as such in the
  README; Dany then asked for and got the two "feasible" ones (security drives, cable glands) plus
  PCB/cable hardware, binder screws and hinges.
- Security screws reuse the ISO head/thread envelopes with only the drive changed (pin carved out of the
  recess tool), so they inherit the verified head dimensions and the cosmetic-thread path; pin/hole sizes
  are representative proportions, stated on each family.
- Fully threaded kinds (DIN 7500, binder screw) use the full shank as cosmetic-thread depth in the add-in;
  everything else keeps the ISO 2d+6 rule. Kinds with the thread at the bottom (cable gland, CD stud,
  M-F pillar stud) and closed-head internal threads (binder post) get no cosmetic thread rather than a
  one-off probe each.
- DIN 7967 is a stamped spring-steel lock nut, not the ISO 7042 type — cross-reference fixed once DIN 980
  existed; do not re-link them.
- `.vs/` (Visual Studio index cache, tracked binaries) was NOT staged in this close-out: it is churn, not
  work. See open threads.

  those families — never run a scoped build while a `FastenerCli.exe convert` live test is running.
- The `test-dialog` screenshot harness (scratchpad only, not committed) intermittently grabs a zero-size
  window; the add-in dialog itself was verified twice with it.
- Remaining TR categories (enclosure locks/latches/gaskets, tooling, castors, medical hardware) are
  deliberately out; representative envelopes of quarter-turn locks would be the next candidate if wanted.
- Several generic families carry "representative, not sourced" dimensions (rivet bush spigot length, hose
  clamp housing length, PT screw K35/K80 heads, all of batch 10) — flagged in each family's `note`.
